# Compliance — legal requirements for phase A

> **Scope rule (BRIEF §4):** this file lists what French/EU law requires for phase A (third-party amicable collection), and nothing more. No certifications, no voluntary frameworks. Anything uncertain goes to [Open questions](#9-open-questions), not into code.
>
> **Sources:** article texts were read on 2026-11-07 from Légifrance via LibreJustice (links below go to LibreJustice, which mirrors Légifrance and links to the official source). Article versions are the ones in force on 2026-11-07 unless stated.
>
> **Status of every row:** *to validate by counsel* (✅ = text verified verbatim; ⚖️ = interpretation needs counsel; ❓ = unverified source).
>
> **Enforcement points** are *planned* names, consistent with `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (Supabase Postgres + RLS + `SECURITY DEFINER` functions; Python worker for the calculation engine, flow executor and channel adapters). They will be renamed if the architecture changes; the test column is the contract.

## Legend for enforcement layers

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DB** | Postgres constraint, trigger or `SECURITY DEFINER` function. Cannot be bypassed by any client. |
| **Engine** | Calculation engine in the worker (`worker/src/collectoo_worker/engine/`). Driven by rule packs. |
| **Guardrail** | Pre-send check in the worker (`worker/src/collectoo_worker/guardrails/.py`), run before every outbound action. Flows cannot skip it. |
| **Template** | Locked legal blocks in the template system (`template_block.locked = true`) plus a render-time validator. |
| **Ops** | Business/organisational obligation. Not code. Tracked here so it isn't forgotten. |

## 1. Third-party amicable collection (CPCE)

Scope: R124-1 applies to anyone collecting debts amicably for third parties, habitually or occasionally ([R124-1](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-des-procedures-civiles-d-execution/r124-1)). Missing an R124-4 mention or breaching R124-2 is a 5th-class contravention ([R124-7](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-des-procedures-civiles-d-execution/r124-7)).

| ID | Requirement | Legal reference | Enforcement point | Proving test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CPCE-01 | Hold professional civil-liability insurance; send a written declaration to the procureur de la République before starting the activity. | [R124-2](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-des-procedures-civiles-d-execution/r124-2) al. 1 and 3 | **Ops.** Not code. | — | ✅ to validate |
| CPCE-02 | Hold a bank account at a credit institution **exclusively** used to receive funds collected for creditors. | [R124-2](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-des-procedures-civiles-d-execution/r124-2) al. 2 | **Ops** (open the account). **DB:** ledger has a distinct `client_money` account class; incoming debtor payments can only post to `client_money` bank accounts (`bank_account.purpose = 'client_funds'`); Collectoo's own revenue (commissions) moves out only via a remittance/commission entry. | pgTAP: inserting a debtor payment on a non-`client_funds` account fails. Property test: Σ client_money balance = Σ(collected) − Σ(remitted) − Σ(commission invoiced). | ✅ to validate |
| CPCE-03 | Collect only after a **written agreement** with the creditor giving power to receive funds on its behalf. | CPCE-05 | Every later collection step reminds the **reference and send date of the first letter**. | [R124-4](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-des-procedures-civiles-d-execution/r124-4), last paragraph | **Template:** locked `first_letter_ref` block mandatory in every subsequent debtor-facing message on every channel (SMS/WhatsApp via a short form + link). **Guardrail:** no outbound collection message for an account until a first letter is recorded as sent; afterwards each message must carry the reference. | Guardrail test: SMS before first letter → refused; SMS after → rendered text contains ref + date. | ✅ ⚖️ (short form for SMS/WhatsApp to validate) |
| CPCE-06 | Inform the creditor of any payment obtained, even partial (unless it comes from an instalment plan the creditor already knows), and, unless agreed otherwise, of any proposal to settle other than by immediate payment. | [R124-5](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-des-procedures-civiles-d-execution/r124-5) | **Worker:** `client_notification` event on every allocated payment and every plan/settlement proposal; delivered via client portal + webhook. Mandate flag `notify_proposals` (default true; can only be turned off if the mandate says so). | Integration test: payment import → client notification row created. | ✅ to validate |
| CPCE-07 | Give the debtor a **receipt (quittance)** for every payment. | [R124-6](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-des-procedures-civiles-d-execution/r124-6) al. 1 | **Worker:** on payment allocation, generate a receipt (amount, date, allocation by component, remaining balance) and send it on the debtor's preferred channel + portal. `payment.receipt_id NOT NULL` once allocated. | Test: allocate payment → receipt artifact exists and is linked; reconciliation job flags any allocated payment without receipt. | ✅ to validate |
| CPCE-08 | Remit funds to the creditor **within one month** of effective collection, unless the agreement says otherwise. | [R124-6](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-des-procedures-civiles-d-execution/r124-6) al. 2 | **DB/Worker:** `mandate.remittance_max_days` (default 30, override only with a mandate clause reference). | CPCE-10 | No document or activity that could be **mistaken for a judicial or bailiff act** or for a public function. | [Code pénal 433-13](https://librejustice.fr/norm/fr/code-penal/433-13) | **Template:** design review of every locked template before publish (manual approval by an internal admin, recorded). ## 3. Holds required by law

All holds are rows in `account_hold` with a `legal_basis` and evidence. The **Guardrail** refuses every outbound collection action (message, plan offer, escalation) on an account with an active blocking hold. Flows cannot override it.

| ID | Requirement | Legal reference | Enforcement point | Proving test | Status |
