# ADR-0014: Remittances, commission, invoices and own cash (extends ADR-0012, ADR-0013)

- Status: Accepted (the funds ledger implemented in M2c-1, identities and terms in M2c-2; engines, runs, invoices, documents and transfers follow in M2c-3 … M2c-7, each adding its section)
- Date: 2026-11-09
- Design: `docs/design/m2c-remittances.md` (three-design panel, critiques and a judge; legal texts re-read on LibreJustice on 2026-11-09). Founder answers on 2026-11-09: all 15 defaults accepted (« All ok », design §24).
- Supersedes ADR-0013's sentence « Only M2c `commission` will be exempt » (from I2): **no entry is exempt** from fund closure.

## Context
M2b books what debtors pay onto the account dedicated to funds collected for creditors (CPCE R124-2) and what each client is owed (4670). M2c pays clients (R124-6, one month), takes the servicer's commission by compensation when the mandate allows it, invoices it, and moves the servicer's own money (fees on the dedicated account, top-ups, sweeps, advances when a remitted collection comes back). The ledger must show at every date whose money is on the dedicated account, never let one client's money serve another client or the servicer (C. pén. 314-1, C. civ. 1993), and keep 5120 equal to the bank (I9).

## Decision

### Two funds and one crossing (design §0, §2 #1, §5)
- **The dedicated-account fund** (`ledger_book.money_of = 'client'`): 5120 `client_money_cash`, 4710 `unallocated_payment`, 4670 `client_payable` and the new **4679 `due_to_servicer`** « Sommes revenant au cabinet sur le compte dédié » (pooled per servicer and currency). −4679 is the servicer's money on the dedicated account at every date; +4679 what the servicer owes it.
- **The servicer's fund** (`money_of = 'servicer'`): **5122 `own_cash`** (one ledger account per `bank_account.purpose = 'own'`; it records only the movements the dedicated account shows and client payments ops prove — it is not the own account's bank balance), **4111 `commission_receivable`**, **4112 `client_advance_receivable`** (per client), **44571 `vat_collected`**, **44574 `vat_pending`**, **4689 `due_from_client_funds`**, 7060 `servicer_revenue` (no longer client-visible), **6270 `bank_fees`**, **6780 `servicer_losses`** (pooled).
- **Every entry closes each fund per currency (I2′)** and **4679 + 4689 = 0 in every entry (I12)**: money crosses between the funds only through this mirror pair, as 1100 / 3100 mirror each other. Checked at commit by `private.ledger_entry_verify` (`LG001`) and fail-fast in `private.ledger_post_set`. I8′ (5120 + 4710 + 4670 + 4679 = 0 per servicer and currency) follows; the nightly checks verify it, the servicer's fund and the mirror over all postings.

### Thirteen entry types with fixed shapes (design §5.2; `ledger_entry_type.allowed_books`, I3′)
`commission_invoice`, `commission_credit_note`, `client_settlement`, `remittance`, `servicer_sweep`, `servicer_top_up`, `bank_fee`, `client_advance`, `servicer_loss`, `client_payment_own`, `client_payment_dedicated`, `client_refund_own`, `advance_write_off`.
- Each has its books and its shape (+ debit / − credit), one currency, checked at commit (`LG001`); with I2′ and I12 the shape fixes every amount (a sweep's four lines are all S). No M2c type touches 1100 or 3100 (L111-8) nor 4710 (unallocated money is never remitted: M2b founder #8, not the law); 7060 is reached only by the two commission types, against the same client's 4111; no M2b type reaches a servicer-fund book or 4679 (I10′).
- Each names its document (`source_type` / `source_id`, C. com. R123-174, CHECK constraint): `commission_invoice`, `remittance_run`, `bank_transfer` or `bank_transaction`, `client_advance`, `document`.
- **Never reversed** (`LG003`): the two commission types (a credit note corrects an invoice), `client_settlement` (it survives a returned payout) and `advance_write_off`. The others are reversed with a motif.
- `private.ledger_post` posts corrections of claims only (`adjustment`); every payment and fund entry goes through `private.ledger_post_set`, which derives the client of client books (4670, 4111, 4112, 7060) and refuses a client on an entry without client lines, and puts 5120 only on an active `client_funds` account and 5122 only on an active `own` account (`LG002`; the ledger account guard too).

### Locks (design §5.5)
- The **allocation lock** `payments:<org>` is taken for every entry with a 4670 line, decided by its lines and not its type (and, as before, for the types that lower a payment or a receivable). With `ledger_reverse`, the only writers of 4670 hold it, so the pending queue read under it is exactly the committed set.
- The **remit lock** `remit:<org>` is taken by every writer of 4679 (`ledger_post_set`, `ledger_reverse` when the reversed entry moves 4679). Order: allocation lock → remit lock → invoice counter (M2c-4).

### Each client's 4670 never in debit, and the pending queue (design §5.4 I13, §9.1)
- `private.client_payable_balance` keeps Σ4670 per (client, currency), all dates, upserted by an ALWAYS statement trigger on `ledger_posting` (one hash join of the statement's new postings with the few 4670 accounts, through a partial index). A deferred constraint trigger refuses a commit leaving it in debit: **new SQLSTATE `LG008`** (Python `ClientMoneyShortError`). A path that forgets to cover a shortfall (M2c-7's advance after a remitted reversal, M2c-6's booking-time cover of a payout overrun) fails at commit instead of using another client's money. `private.ledger_check_now()` runs the ledger's commit checks inside a call (the debit job then sends a refused line to ops). The deferred checks run as their owner (a command's commit runs them as the API's role).
- The same trigger feeds **`private.remittance_pending`**: every entry that moves 4670 and is a remittance item — `allocation`, `forward`, their reversals, `client_advance` and its reversal, the reversal of a `remittance` (`payout_returned`); M2c-6 inserts unplanned payouts itself. Settlements and planned remittances are not items. Amount: what the entry adds to what the client is owed. A run (M2c-4) reserves its items and deletes them when it freezes its lines. Back-filled by the migration (none in production). Nightly: I13 (running balance = postings, never in debit) and, before runs exist, I14 (what each client is owed = its pending items).

### Bank accounts (design §2 #13, §5.3; founder #14)
- `bank_account.purpose` gains **`own`**: the servicer's own account, no statement feed (its statements are not imported: a statement row on it is refused). A second **active** `client_funds` account in a currency is refused by `register_bank_account` (a servicer that already had two keeps them; M2c-4 blocks its runs); one account per currency is the founder's choice, not a rule of R124-2. An IBAN is registered for one purpose only, in any currency: the dedicated account is never also the servicer's own account. `close_bank_account` refuses an account with a transfer in flight from or to it.

### Our own transfers at import (design §12)
- New `bank_transaction.kind`s: **`transfer_out`** (a debit carrying our `RMT…` / `SWP…` end-to-end id, or to a registered own IBAN), **`own_transfer_in`** (a credit carrying our `TOP…` id, or from a registered own IBAN — **never automatically a debtor payment**: ops book it as a top-up or release it as a debtor's payment the servicer relays, below), **`client_transfer_in`** (a credit from a client's registered payout IBAN; the list is empty until M2c-2 registers payout accounts). Our ids are the prefix and exactly 12 upper-case hex digits (`TOPAZ-…` stays a debtor's). camt.053 `NtryDtls/Btch/PmtInfId` is kept (`payment_info_id`, for M2c-6's batch bookings).
- The worker's parsers classify with the servicer's IBANs (`private.statement_classification_ibans`); `private.ingest_statement` classifies again under its lock with the SQL twin `private.bank_line_refined_kind` (a parity test compares them) and keeps the stage rows in step, so a stale list never makes a payment of our own money, and a held duplicate keeps that reading.

### Bank lines booked in the funds (design §6.1 `bank_line_booking`, §12, §13)
- `bank_line_booking(bank_transaction_id, seq, kind, transfer_id, entry_id, amount_minor, method, reason, decided_by)`: one bank line → n entries of the funds; append-only; a guard checks the line, the entry (type, date, bank account, document) and the transfer; at commit Σ bookings + the line's M2b `cash_out` (if any) = the line's amount. `bank_unposted` (I9) counts booked lines as in the ledger.
- **Bank fees** (`bank_fee`: +4679 / −5120 ‖ +6270 / −4689): the servicer's cost (founder #9), never a client's. The debit job books automatically a debit the bank codes as its charge (camt.053 BkTxCd sub-family `CHRG` or `FEES`) — M2b fee lines included, the migration queues the job; ops book other debits as fees (« Frais bancaires »). Only a line read as an other debit is a fee: our transfers out never are (M2c-6 books them).
- **Top-ups** (`servicer_top_up`: +5120 / −4679 ‖ +4689 / −5122): ops order one from the own account (`request_top_up`, a `bank_transfer` of kind `top_up` with our `TOP…` id, typed in the bank; `cancel_top_up` while pending). The debit job books the credit carrying that id, of that amount, on that dedicated account; ops book a credit from a registered own IBAN that no order matches. The automatic top-up file is M2c-7.
- **A debtor's payment relayed by the servicer** (design §12, ledger critique #8; review fix): a debtor paid the servicer's own account and the servicer passes the money on to the dedicated account. `public.release_own_credit(key, line, reason)` (admin or ops manager at aal2, the reason required and kept) takes only an unbooked, unlinked `own_transfer_in` credit on an active `client_funds` account of the caller's servicer and books it as a debtor payment, as a released duplicate credit is: a `payment` (payer « Versement relayé par le cabinet », `unmatched`, review `no_match`, so it waits in « Paiements à traiter » for ops to assign it) and its `cash_in` at the line's booking date. Refusals: `not_own_credit`, `already_booked`, `already_linked`, `reason_required` (`23514`). Whether the earlier receipt on the own account matters for R124-2 and R124-6 is OQ-69.
- **The servicer's loss** (`servicer_loss`: +4679 / −5120 ‖ +6780 / −4689; OQ-34, founder #8): a return larger than what is left of its payment is linked by ops as the payment's M2b return of what is left (its allocations reversed at the return date) plus a loss booking of the excess. Never charged to the client or another client; recovery from the payer is an ops matter.
- Our transfers out and credits from a client are left to ops with why (`bank_debit_review`: `top_up_not_found`, `top_up_differs`, `client_credit`, `own_transfer_out`) until M2c-6 / M2c-7 book them; meanwhile ops may explain them (an I9 gap, visible). They are never linked as a payment's return or our refund (`not_a_return`), and « À classer » does not offer it.

### Read access (design §6.2; compliance critique C6)
Every book without a portfolio is named in the policies: the two funds' books are read by admins and ops managers at aal2; a client reads only its own 4670, and of the entries without a portfolio only its own `forward`, `reversal`, `remittance`, `client_settlement` and `client_advance`. Collectors and clients no longer read 7060 (the `staff_org_ids` / `client_visible` path is closed). `bank_transfer` and `bank_line_booking`: money staff; the running balance and the queue: nobody through the API.

## Consequences
- 5120 stays equal to the bank: fees and top-ups are booked from the bank's lines, not estimated; the acceptance « 5120 = bank after fees and top-ups » is checked end to end (`worker/tests/test_funds_integration.py`).
- Whatever the servicer owes the dedicated account (a fee, a loss, later an advance) is visible on 4679 at once and nets against the servicer's settled commission before a sweep (M2c-6) or is covered by a top-up.
- **Until M2c-7 wires the advance into every reversal path, a reversal of a remitted entry fails closed with `LG008` only when it would leave the client's whole 4670 in debit** (the check is on the client's balance per currency, all entries). When the client has other collections pending, the reversal nets against them in the pending queue, the silent compensation design §13 #1 and founder #7 exclude. M2c-7 brings the advance (`cover_remitted_reversals`) and its nightly backstop; until it is merged there is no real remittance (design §21), and no M2c-1 command posts a `remittance`.
- Cost: the 4670 trigger adds one pass over each posting statement's new rows (measured in `docs/benchmarks/remittances.md`); the per-entry commit check adds one keyed pass for I2′ / I12.

## Review fixes (M2c-1 review, 2026-11-09)
Confirmed findings of the five-lens review (security, money, legal, tests, performance), fixed in this slice:
- **One decision per bank line** (security #0, money #0): the debit links (M2b path) and the funds' bookings (M2c path) take a common lock on the line before they read whether it is linked or booked, so a line is never both linked and booked (5120 never taken down twice).
- **A debtor's payment relayed by the servicer** (money #1, legal #0): `public.release_own_credit` (above); the copy no longer says a credit from the own account is never a debtor's payment, only that it is never taken for one automatically (OQ-69).
- **One purpose per IBAN** (legal #2): `register_bank_account` refuses an IBAN already registered for this servicer with the other purpose, in any currency.
- **Top-up alert** (legal #3): a nightly alert (`servicer_shortfall`, design §15) when the servicer owes the dedicated account (4679 in debit) beyond the top-ups ordered.
- **No fee booking of our transfers out** (legal #5): `fee` only on a line read as an other debit (the guard keeps `return` for M2c-6's charges on a returned payout).
- **Plan robustness** (performance #0–#2, ARCHITECTURE §4): the funds pass's anti-joins on `bank_line_booking` and `bank_debit_link` and its lookups of `bank_duplicate_resolution` and `bank_transfer` are keyed probes (`OFFSET 0`, no `LIMIT 1` over a filtered join); `post_bank_bookings` reads the chunk's lines by keyed laterals and locks the bank accounts from them. The deferred `LG008` check fires only when a client's running balance goes into debit, no longer once per upsert when a loop posts single entries (performance #3: 10,000 single-row upserts 1.57 s → 0.46 ms of checks); the upserts themselves stay one per posting statement (a set-based `ledger_reverse`, one upsert per client and chunk, is left for when return volumes need it: ~0.19 s per 4,000 reversals of one client).
- **Top-up lock** (security #1): `request_top_up` locks both accounts and checks them after the lock, so a concurrent close cannot leave a top-up in flight on a closed account.
- **Decided lines leave the queues** (tests #0, #2): a return booked entirely as the servicer's loss is no longer a debit candidate nor reviewed again by the job; a fee already booked is no longer listed in « À classer » as a possible refund.
- **Stronger pgTAP 017** (tests #3): client reads scoped client by client (A/B) and for a portfolio-restricted viewer — which found the client branch of the ledger read policies ignored portfolio restrictions: a member restricted to some portfolios no longer reads the client's 4670, client-level entries or `ledger_balance` (`private.client_whole_org_ids`, OQ-48 default); `fund_mirror` and `own_fund_identity` shown to fire on tampered postings.
- **« À classer »** (tests #1): no « Retour du paiement » nor refund link on our transfers out, our own credits or a client's credit (the database refuses them, `not_a_return`).
- **Law and our own rules apart** (legal #1, #6): the `LG008` consequence above states when it fails closed; « un seul compte dédié par devise » is the founder's choice (#14), not R124-2; COMPLIANCE CPCE-02 grounds only 1100/3100 on L111-8 (4710 is M2b founder #8).

Refuted at verification: M2b fee lines stuck as « expliquées » (money #2: M2b left them unlinked, so the job books the coded ones); a cap on manual top-ups (legal #4: neither the law nor the design asks for one, OQ-35).

## M2c-2: identities and terms (CPCE-03, M2-13 data; design §6.1–§6.2, §8.1–§8.2, §8.5, §9.1, §10, §14.2, §17)
Migration `20261111120000_identities_terms.sql` (forward-only; redefines `confirm_boarding_batch`, `complete_batch_confirmation`, `receipt_prepare`, `registered_client_ibans`, `close_bank_account` with their settings and grants).
- **Billing identity** (`billing_identity`, one per organization, the servicer and each client; legal name and SIREN stay on `organization`): legal form and capital, RCS city, registered office and billing address (`{line1, line2, postcode, city, country}`), VAT number (French key checked against the SIREN), professional, `established_in` derived from the registered office (mainland France: a French postcode outside 97x/98x; Corsica is mainland), in liquidation, EI (derived from the form), the client's acceptance of PDF invoices (CGI 289 VI). The servicer's is saved complete or not at all — SIREN, a legal form R123-238 lets us print (a servicer of another form cannot save it), the capital for SARL and sociétés par actions, RCS city, VAT number — because every document prints it. `set_billing_identity` (admin at aal2, compare and set on `version`). Staff read every identity of the servicer; a client member reads its own and its servicer's.
- **Billing settings** (`servicer_billing_settings`): invoice prefix (2–6 capitals), the own account due invoices are paid to (an active own account; `close_bank_account` refuses it while named), VAT on debits (242 nonies A 11° bis), the penalty text (default: the ECB rate + 10 points, L441-10 II; a free text until invoices print it: the « ≥ 3 × the legal rate » floor of an agreed rate is checked in M2c-5 with M3's legal-rate table), the bank cut-off per dedicated account. Money staff read, admins write.
- **Client documents**: the RIB, the signed mandate or an amendment, the evidence of an event, in the private bucket `documents` at `client/<servicer>/<client>/<uuid>.<pdf|png|jpg>`, uploaded by money staff from the browser (storage policy: that key, a new one) and registered with their SHA-256 (`register_client_document`). Read by money staff; the RIB and the mandate also by the client's administrators with access to the whole client (OQ-48 default); the evidence by money staff only. Served after the hash check.
- **Payout accounts under dual control** (founder #10; design §2.2 #16): `client_payout_account` holds the IBAN, BIC and holder as on the client's RIB (the payee name the bank checks). Money staff propose (`propose_payout_account`; never one of the servicer's own IBANs, never another client's current or proposed IBAN, one proposal at a time per client and currency). A **second person** confirms by typing the IBAN read on the RIB (compare and set): the client's administrator with access to the whole client, at aal2, in the client space, or another admin of the servicer at aal2; never the proposer (command and CHECK constraint), never an ops manager. A court-appointed account (the liquidator's, L641-9 I) is confirmed by a second admin only, never by the client's users (OQ-41 default). A confirmed account replaces the current one (one current account per client and currency); `reject_payout_account` (money staff or the client's administrator) and `retire_payout_account` (admin) need a reason; what was proposed never changes and the status only moves forward (ALWAYS guard). The page flags an active IBAN no payout was booked to yet (« premier virement à vérifier »); M2c-4 shows it on the run. Client users step up to aal2 on `/mfa` (opened to them, back to the page they came from). `private.registered_client_ibans` now returns every confirmed IBAN (current, returned or retired), so a credit from one is read as `client_transfer_in` at import — by the parser and by `ingest_statement` — and never as a debtor payment; a proposal is not yet the client's.
- **Client status events** (`client_status_event`, append-only, with evidence): proceeding opened, liquidation, death, guardianship, mandate ended (C. civ. 2003, C. com. L622-7, L641-9); for a liquidation the liquidator's court-appointed account may be named. Their effects on runs and payouts come with M2c-4 and M2c-7 (OQ-41).
- **Mandate money terms** (R124-3 2°–4°): `mandate.insurance_clause_ref` (2°, `set_mandate_insurance_clause`, compare and set); per `mandate_terms` version the commission schedule (`commission-1`) and the remittance terms (`remittance-1`), each with its clause reference **in a column** (`commission_clause_ref`, `remittance_clause_ref`: one place, not repeated inside the documents; the netting and claw-back clauses are named inside the remittance terms), the signed document and `schedule_sha256`. The delay stays `remittance_max_days` (1–30), the one source. Validators: SQL (`private.commission_schedule_error`, `private.remittance_terms_error`, also CHECK constraints) and pydantic (`collectoo_worker.remittance.terms`; the JSON Schemas in `remittance/schemas/` are generated from the models and a test keeps them in step); both give the same verdict on 5,000 generated schedules and 5,000 generated terms, valid and invalid. Amounts and rates are strings; whole-rate tiers are not a kind; the last rule matches everything; ids and tier keys are distinct; a weekly frequency names a weekday 1–5; a due invoice is payable within 45 days (L441-10 I); `holdback` must be empty until M5.
- **Versions** (§6.1): a version in force (from ≤ Paris today) never changes — every column but its end, compared as a whole row so a later column is covered too — its end moves only to a day not yet passed, and it is never deleted; a version not yet in force may be superseded. A second version never starts before today (never retroactive; a mandate's first version may start at its signature). `set_mandate_terms(key, mandate, valid_from ≥ today, schedule, clause, terms, clause, document, max_days, expected_latest_id)` (admin at aal2): compare and set on the mandate's latest version as the page showed it; the version covering the day ends then, versions starting on or after it are superseded, the new one keeps the mandate's end and the covering version's plan envelope and settlement authority; claim types it names exist and portfolios it names are the mandate's. A mandate with versions is never deleted (`ON DELETE RESTRICT`). Back-fill: versions written before M2c-2 get one flat rule at their `commission_rate` and no clause references, so they read « incomplete » (none in production).
- **Confirmation gate** (§9.1; ops critique #3, compliance critique B8): `private.portfolio_confirmation_blockers` / `batch_confirmation_blockers` name, in order: `no_active_mandate`, `insurance_clause_missing`, `commission_schedule_missing`, `commission_clause_missing`, `remittance_terms_missing`, `remittance_clause_missing`, `terms_document_missing`, `servicer_identity_incomplete`, `client_identity_incomplete`, `client_not_professional`, `client_not_mainland_france`, `currency_not_eur`. `confirm_boarding_batch` refuses with the list; the confirmation job checks again (an identity or terms may change in between) and sends the batch back to `committed` with the reasons, nothing posted. The batch page lists them before « Confirmer »; `/backoffice/clients` and the client page show them per portfolio (`client_readiness`).
- **Receipts** (founder 2026-11-09, R123-237/238): `private.receipt_issuer` builds the issuer block from the identity; every new version's payload carries it and the worker renders template `quittance-fr-3` (the block above the parties). « What the receipt says changed » leaves the issuer out, so a new or changed identity alone never issues a version: receipts issued before keep `quittance-fr-2` and their bytes (its golden is unchanged; `quittance-fr-3` has its own). A stored receipt is regenerated with its own template version.
- **Pages** (French, phone-first): `/backoffice/parametres/facturation` (identity with the receipt block previewed, settings), `/backoffice/clients` (readiness per client) and `/backoffice/clients/[id]/reversement` (readiness, identity, documents, payout IBAN, mandates and their versions in words, events), `/client/mandat` (terms in words, « Confirmer le nouvel IBAN »; restricted users read that their access does not cover it). The terms are entered as structured fields plus the schedule as JSON, shown back in words; the editor with live examples from the engine (§17.4) comes with M2c-3's engine.

## Founder decisions (2026-11-09, all defaults; design §24)
Twice-monthly runs with a deadline guard (#1); the shorter of the mandate's delay and one calendar month (#2); netting only under a clause (#3); commission on allocations only (#4); the supported grids (#5); credit notes for undone collections (#6); advances, claw-back only by clause, no hold-back (#7); **the servicer bears a return beyond its payment (#8)**; **the commission swept the day payouts are debited, bank fees on the dedicated account the servicer's cost (#9)**; IBAN changes confirmed by a second person (#10); one approver (#11); one yearly invoice series (#12); mainland-France professional clients in EUR (#13); **one dedicated account per currency (#14)**; pain.001 files uploaded by ops, the payee check kept (#15).
