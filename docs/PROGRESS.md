- 2026-11-09 M2b-6 (ADR-0013 addendum, where the design was open): nothing leaves the client-funds cash without the bank's line (a refund's « manual confirmation » is ops linking its debit); one pending refund per payment, reason codes matching its state, the payer's IBAN by default; automatic return links only for a full return of the one payment the line names, everything else to ops; a partial return reverses every allocation at the return date and the rest is allocated again from that date; the client is told of returns, not of refunds; links are final; direct payments go through the waterfall and are allowed during a hold (OQ-31); the design's `import_correction` (a wrong CSV line already booked) is not built (duplicates are held before booking).
- 2026-11-09 M2b-4 (ADR-0013 addendum, where the design was open): the allocation job is queued by a trigger whenever a payment becomes ready; reversal, forward and credit note post at once under the allocation lock (2 s, then « réessayez »), decisions needing the waterfall go through the job; a reversed payment waits for ops; a file with a credit note to compensate holds its payments until ops apply it (or allocate without it); forward only when nothing was allocated; an overpayment pays another file only of the same client and debtor, on a filed request; policies never retroactive; items without a due date count as due after the dated ones; consumer mortgages are consumer loans for founder #6.
- 2026-11-09 M2b-3 (ADR-0013 addendum, where the design was open): two files named is never automatic (every cited number must name the same single file; an RF of one file with a number of another goes to ops); an RF whose check digits fail is never guessed from; the IBAN memory is taught only by RF, backed invoice numbers and ops decisions, and forgets what an unassignment undoes; a payment ops decided by hand only gets suggestions afterwards; one file per payment until allocation (splitting with M2b-4); releasing a CSV suspected duplicate moves to M2b-6.
- 2026-11-08 M2b-2 (ADR-0013 addendum, where the design was open): an unsplit batch credit is one payment booked at import (a bank line is at most one payment); credits that undo a debit, debits and CSV suspected duplicates are recorded, not booked (M2b-3, M2b-6); one statement format per bank account (CAMT and CSV keys differ); a CSV bank id only under an unambiguous header (« Référence opération »); a reused bank reference with other facts refuses the file instead of being skipped.
- 2026-11-08 payments (founder: all 20 defaults accepted, design §17, ADR-0013): money on the dedicated account is booked at statement import in pooled "to be allocated" money; one RF reference per file at confirmation (existing files back-filled); automatic match only on an RF reference or a corroborated invoice/file number or a known IBAN, never on name + amount; legal order locked (debtor's designation, then due debts oldest first, interest before principal); overpayments never passed to the client, refunded to the payer; no go-live with real payments before M3 computes interest; synthetic bank files only until anonymised real exports arrive.

## Open questions
- Returns after a remittance (design risk R4, OQ-25): M2b-6 reverses allocations at the return date; once M2c remits, a return can make 4670[client] negative — M2c must hold back or claw back. Refunds to another account than the payer's (OQ-30) and payments made to the creditor during a hold (OQ-31) keep conservative defaults until counsel answers.
- Refunds are executed by ops in the bank with the `RFD…` reference (no pain.001 file yet): generate one with the M2c remittance files?
- Receipts (OQ-22 open): the issuer's address is not on the receipt (organizations have no address yet); the receipt's content is the design's conservative list. Storage: ~87 KB per receipt uncompressed (~4.3 GB per 50k): compress once determinism is checked independently of zlib, or lighten the font subsets.
- Designated debts among themselves follow the legal order (oldest due first, equal due dates pro rata), not the order written — founder to confirm (default: legal order).
- Allocation at scale (design risk R1): the per-entry checks at commit cost ~0.3 ms per allocation (within the design's estimate), but the whole posting path (`ledger_post_set`: entry checks, inserts, foreign keys; payment row triggers) puts 50k allocations at ~45–50 s in the background job, above the design's 20–30 s. Fallback when volumes need it: statement-level checks (R1) and a cheaper request check in `ledger_post_set`.
- Reconciliation calibration (decision #19): the 95 % is on synthetic statements; real French remittance texts may cite references differently (the design's risk R5).
